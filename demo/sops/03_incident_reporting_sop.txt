INCIDENT REPORTING AND ESCALATION SOP
Document code: OPS-011
Version: 1.4
Department: Operations
Effective date: 2025-06-01
Review date: 2026-06-01

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines how staff report, classify and escalate an operational
incident so that severe events reach management without delay.

2. SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION
2.1 Severity 1 is any incident that stops service delivery at a facility, or
involves a risk to life, or involves loss of client data.
2.2 Severity 2 is any incident that degrades service delivery, or affects more
than one facility, or affects more than twenty clients.
2.3 Severity 3 is any other incident with limited or no impact on service
delivery.

3. REPORTING TIMELINES
3.1 A Severity 1 incident must be reported immediately by telephone and confirmed
in writing within one hour.
3.2 A Severity 2 incident must be reported in writing within four hours.
3.3 A Severity 3 incident must be reported in writing within two working days.

4. ESCALATION PATH
4.1 Severity 3 incidents are handled by the facility supervisor.
4.2 Severity 2 incidents must be escalated to the district operations manager.
4.3 Severity 1 incidents must be escalated to the national operations director
and to the Data Protection Officer where client data is involved.

5. INVESTIGATION
5.1 Every Severity 1 and Severity 2 incident requires a written investigation
report within ten working days of the incident.
5.2 The report must state the root cause and the corrective actions taken.
5.3 Corrective actions must be assigned an owner and a due date.

6. COMMUNICATION
6.1 Affected clients must be informed of a Severity 1 incident within 48 hours.
6.2 Internal communication must not attribute blame to an individual before the
investigation is complete.

7. REVIEW
7.1 All Severity 1 incidents must be reviewed at the monthly management meeting.
