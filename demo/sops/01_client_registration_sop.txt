CLIENT REGISTRATION SOP
Document code: CLN-001
Version: 3.2
Department: Clinical Services
Effective date: 2025-01-15
Review date: 2026-01-15

1. PURPOSE
This procedure describes how facility staff register a new client and how the
client record is verified, stored and protected. It applies to every client
contact where a clinical service is delivered.

2. SCOPE
This SOP applies to registration officers, clinical services staff and
supervisors at all facilities. It does not cover billing or insurance claims.

3. REGISTRATION PROCEDURE
3.1 Every new client must present a valid government-issued identification
document. Where identification is unavailable, a supervisor must authorise
registration in writing before any clinical service is provided.
3.2 The registration officer must capture the client's full name, date of birth,
sex and contact telephone number.
3.3 A unique client identification number must be generated by the system before
any clinical service is provided.
3.4 The registration officer must record the client's consent to data processing
in the client file.
3.5 Registration records must be verified by a supervisor within 24 hours of
creation.
3.6 Duplicate records must not be created. Where a duplicate is suspected, the
registration officer must search the register by name and date of birth first.

4. MISSED APPOINTMENTS
4.1 If a client misses a scheduled appointment, the registration officer must
attempt telephone contact within two working days.
4.2 The officer must record the outcome of the contact attempt in the client file.
4.3 After three consecutive missed appointments, the case must be referred to the
clinical supervisor for review.

5. DATA PROTECTION
5.1 Client records are confidential and may only be accessed by authorised staff
acting within their duties.
5.2 Client records must be retained for seven years after the last clinical
contact, after which they are securely destroyed.
5.3 Any suspected unauthorised access to client records must be reported to the
Data Protection Officer within 24 hours.

6. TRAINING AND COMPETENCY
6.1 Registration officers must complete induction training before registering
clients without supervision.
6.2 Competency must be reassessed annually.
