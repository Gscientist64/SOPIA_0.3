DATA QUALITY AND MONTHLY REPORTING SOP
Document code: MER-004
Version: 2.0
Department: Monitoring and Evaluation
Effective date: 2025-03-01
Review date: 2026-03-01

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines how programme data is collected, verified, corrected and
reported each month, and who is accountable at each step.

2. DEFINITIONS
2.1 A data element is a single field recorded at the point of service delivery.
2.2 A data quality check is the comparison of recorded values against the source
document.
2.3 A variance is any difference greater than five per cent between reported and
verified values.

3. DATA COLLECTION
3.1 Field officers must record data on the day the service is delivered.
3.2 Paper registers must be transcribed into the system within three working days.
3.3 Data must not be estimated. Where a value is unknown, it must be recorded as
unknown rather than left blank or guessed.

4. VERIFICATION
4.1 The M&E officer must verify a random sample of at least ten per cent of records
each month.
4.2 Verification must be documented, naming the verifier and the date.
4.3 Where a variance greater than five per cent is found, a corrective action log
must be opened.

5. CORRECTIONS
5.1 Corrections must never overwrite the original value. Both the original and
corrected value must remain visible with the reason for change.
5.2 The reason for any correction must be recorded.
5.3 Correction requests must be approved by the M&E officer.

6. REPORTING TIMELINES
6.1 Facility reports are due by the fifth working day of the following month.
6.2 District reports are due by the tenth working day.
6.3 National reports are due by the fifteenth working day.
6.4 Late submissions must be accompanied by a written explanation.

7. DATA RETENTION
7.1 Programme data must be retained for ten years.
